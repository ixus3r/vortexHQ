# What's new in Vortex

A short tour of recent desktop capabilities. This page describes product highlights, not a complete version-by-version changelog. For the current build, use the [official downloads](https://vortexhq.dev/#download) and [update guide](https://vortexhq.dev/docs/updates-and-downloads).

## Design before you build

For a new screen or a larger visual change, review a design in the canvas before moving into implementation. Keep the design attached to the conversation so the next change has a clear reference.

[Explore designs](https://vortexhq.dev/docs/design-mode-and-agent-designs)

## Ask your agent for a video

Turn a brief into a video request using a compatible model from your connected OpenRouter account. The workflow checks an estimated cost against your budget and brings the result back for review. Provider charges and supported inputs depend on the model.

[Agent video guide](https://vortexhq.dev/docs/agent-video-generation)

## More choice of coding agent

Use the agent that suits the task. The lineup includes Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, OpenCode, Google Antigravity, Grok Build, Kimi Code and Muse Code, with your own provider accounts.

[Connection guides](https://vortexhq.dev/docs)

## Keep passwords and secure notes close

The personal vault keeps logins and notes encrypted and associated with your Vortex account.

[Passwords and secrets](https://vortexhq.dev/docs/passwords-and-secrets)

## See the work across your workspace

Follow running tasks, requests for input and unread results from the workspace rail. Use linked conversations for parallel work and Resource Manager to inspect supported local tasks and processes.

[Parallel tasks](https://vortexhq.dev/docs/linked-project-tasks) · [Resource Manager](https://vortexhq.dev/docs/resource-manager)
